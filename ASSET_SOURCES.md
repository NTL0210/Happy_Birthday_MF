# ASSET_SOURCES — Moonlight Protocol: Strawberry Dream

Asset provenance and licensing register. **No external asset has been selected,
downloaded, or verified yet.** All rows below are candidates or policy notes, not
cleared assets.

## Rules for this register

- An asset is recorded only after its **original source page** has been opened and
  its license inspected at a reliable URL.
- "Free to view" is not "free to redistribute". Licenses must permit the intended
  static-site use and any required attribution must be satisfiable.
- Do not mark an asset `VERIFIED` without a license name, license URL, and source URL.
- Do not use AI image generators. Small handcrafted SVG/CSS is permitted for
  interface geometry and tiny decorations, not as a substitute for researched major
  illustrations.
- No proprietary Atlus/SEGA screenshots, logos, character art, portraits, or music.
- Record any modification (cropping, recoloring, optimization) and whether the license
  allows it.
- Prefer a small, coherent set over many unrelated assets.

## Verification status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `CANDIDATE` | Source found; license not yet inspected |
| `VERIFIED` | Original license page inspected; use and redistribution permitted |
| `ATTRIBUTION REQUIRED` | Verified, but credit must be displayed |
| `BLOCKED` | License insufficient or unclear; must not ship |
| `FALLBACK` | Coherent non-AI substitute created in-house; documented |
